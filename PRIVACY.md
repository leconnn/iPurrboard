# iPurrboard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

iPurrboard has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. What you type stays on your phone. The
keyboard only goes online to check for updates (once a day, which you can turn off) and for features
you turn on: Emoji Kitchen stickers.

## What you type

- Your typing is processed on your phone to show suggestions and corrections. It is never sent
  anywhere and never leaves the device.
- To improve suggestions, the keyboard remembers words you type that aren't in its dictionary,
  which words you type after which, and corrections you undo. You can see and delete these in
  Settings > Learned Words.
- Nothing is learned in password fields, or in fields where the app asks keyboards not to learn
  (such as a browser's incognito tab).

## Clipboard and screenshots

- With Clipboard History on, text you copy is kept on your phone so you can paste it again. Items
  are deleted after the time you choose (1 hour by default) unless pinned. Copies from password or
  incognito fields, and text marked sensitive (for example by password managers), are never kept.
- Show Copied Items reads the clipboard when the keyboard opens, to offer what you just copied.
- With Save Recent Screenshots on (and photo access allowed), the keyboard looks for new screenshots
  in your photo library to offer them in the clipboard. They stay in your library and are never
  uploaded; one is only copied when you paste it into an app.

## Voice typing

- Dictation uses your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's). Your voice is handled
  by that service under its own privacy policy, which may process it online. iPurrboard only
  receives the text and does not store any audio.

## Features that go online

This is off until you turn it on. When used, your phone connects directly to the service,
which receives your IP address and the request.

- **Emoji Kitchen stickers:** sticker pictures are loaded from Google's servers (gstatic.com), so
  Google can see which emoji combinations are shown. Google's privacy policy applies:
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.
- Sticker pictures are cached on your phone for a short time to load faster.

## Software updates

- Once a day, iPurrboard asks GitHub (github.com, where it is published) whether a newer version
  exists. GitHub receives your IP address and an ordinary request; nothing about you or what you type
  is sent. GitHub's privacy statement applies. Turn it off in Settings > Software Update >
  Automatic Checks.
- An update is only downloaded when you tap Download and Install, and Android asks you to confirm
  before it is installed.

## What is stored, and where

- Settings, learned words, clipboard history, emoji recents and text replacements are stored only
  on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not included in Android backups.
- You can delete them anytime: Settings > Learned Words > Reset Learned Data, Clear in the
  clipboard, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** only for voice typing, asked the first time you use it.
- **Photos:** only for Save Recent Screenshots, asked when you turn it on.
- **Internet:** for update checks and Emoji Kitchen stickers.
- **Install apps:** only to install an update you chose to download; Android asks you to allow it once.
- **Vibration:** for key feedback.

## No sharing or selling

iPurrboard does not collect personal data, so it has nothing to share or sell. There are no
analytics, crash reporting, ads or third-party trackers in the app.

## Children

iPurrboard does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at
